# Editorial Asset Provenance

Tool: built-in image generation (not a TrendZ runtime provider).

Saved project asset: `static/images/beauty-editorial-triptych.png`.
Runtime use: decorative skincare/makeup/haircare imagery on trend and planner cards.
It is not source evidence, a catalogue product photograph, a brand endorsement or
a depiction of measured activity. The selected-product panel uses a package icon
instead of pretending that editorial artwork depicts its named product.

No image generation, LLM, external source ingestion or provider call occurs in
TrendZ at runtime. The asset is served locally. Original generation output was
copied without editing from the built-in generator's saved image.

## Prompt

Create one wide editorial beauty photography triptych, three equal-width panels
side by side with clean straight divisions, approximately 3:1 overall ratio.
Intended as non-branded category illustrations for a serious beauty-market
intelligence dashboard, not product or measured evidence. Left panel: close-up
of clear glass skincare serum pipette and a clean translucent gel swatch on a pale
cool sage studio surface, beautiful realistic glass refraction, restrained. Middle
panel: raspberry lip tint smear with an unbranded minimal lipstick tube and small
makeup brush on a very pale blush studio surface. Right panel: amber hair oil in a
plain clear bottle beside a smooth dark strand of hair and simple black comb on a
pale grey studio surface. Bright natural studio daylight, sophisticated cosmetic
editorial photography, real material texture, sharply inspectable objects, no
atmospheric haze, no bokeh, no gradients, no decorations, no flowers, no people or
faces, no words, no labels, no logos, no typography, no watermarks. All three
compositions have interesting objects spanning most of their panel, not empty
expanses. Premium modern minimalist palette, subtle contrast between panels.
Output image only.
